**## CS101 Potential Project Ideas

### Tip Calculator (*Android App*)
 - **Inputs**: User types in the ***Bill***, ***Tip Percentage***, and ***Number of People***, then hits a button that says **SUBMIT**
 - **Logic Required**: Calculate the ***Tip Amount***, ***Total Bill***, and ***Per Person Bill***
 - **Outputs**: The app prints the ***Tip Amount***, ***Total Bill***, and ***Per Person Bill***

### Flash Cards (*Android App*)
 - **Inputs**: User sees a ***Term*** and has three buttons: **Show Answer**, **Next**, and **Random**
 - **Logic Required**: Track the ***Current Card***, reveal the ***Answer*** when requested, go to the ***Next Card*** in order or pick a ***Random Card***
 - **Outputs**: The app displays the ***Term***, and when **Show Answer** is pressed, reveals the ***Definition***

### Quiz App - Multiple Choice (*CLI or Android App*)
 - **Inputs**: User sees a ***Question*** with multiple ***Answer Choices*** and selects one
 - **Logic Required**: Check if the selected answer matches the ***Correct Answer***, track ***Score*** and ***Question Number***
 - **Outputs**: The app tells the user if they were ***Correct*** or ***Incorrect*** after each question, and displays their ***Final Score*** at the end

### Multiplication Practice Game (*CLI or Android App*)
 - **Inputs**: User is presented with a ***Multiplication Problem*** and types in their ***Answer***
 - **Logic Required**: Generate two ***Random Numbers***, calculate the ***Correct Product***, compare to the user's answer, track ***Score***
 - **Outputs**: The app tells the user if they were ***Correct*** or ***Incorrect***, and displays their ***Final Score*** at the end

### CLI Story Game (*CLI*)
 - **Inputs**: User reads a ***Scenario*** and chooses between ***Option 1*** or ***Option 2*** by typing **1** or **2**
 - **Logic Required**: Use ***if/else*** statements to branch the story based on the user's choices, track ***Health***, ***Supplies***, or other stats across scenarios. Some of the choice outcomes are based on probability (some random chance)
 - **Outputs**: The app prints the ***Story Text*** for each scenario, the ***Result*** of each choice, and an ***Ending*** based on how the game plays out (e.g. survive or game over)

### Dice Rolling Game (*CLI or Android App*)
 - **Inputs**: Two players take turns pressing a **Roll** button (Android) or hitting **enter** (cli) to roll the dice
 - **Logic Required**: Generate ***Random Numbers*** for each die roll, calculate each player's ***Total***, track ***Rounds***, compare scores, and determine the ***Winner***
 - **Outputs**: The app displays each player's ***Roll Value***, their ***Running Score***, and announces the ***Winner*** at the end

### Rock Paper Scissors (*CLI or Android App*)
 - **Inputs**: User selects ***Rock***, ***Paper***, or ***Scissors*** by typing or pressing a button
 - **Logic Required**: Generate a ***Random Choice*** for the computer, compare both choices to determine ***Win***, ***Lose***, or ***Tie***, track ***Wins***, ***Losses***, and ***Ties*** across rounds
 - **Outputs**: The app displays the ***Computer's Choice***, the ***Result*** of each round, and a ***Running Scoreboard*****

### Pokémon Battle (*CLI or Android App*)
 - **Inputs**: User selects a ***Move*** from a list of available attacks by typing a number or pressing a button
 - **Logic Required**: Extend the Pikachu Battle from HW4 — add more ***Pokémon*** to choose from, each with unique ***Moves***, ***Health***, and ***Stats***. Use ***Random Numbers*** for damage ranges and miss chances, track ***HP*** for both sides, and determine when a Pokémon ***Faints***
 - **Outputs**: The app displays each turn's ***Attack***, whether it ***Hit*** or ***Missed***, the ***Damage*** dealt, both Pokémon's ***Remaining HP***, and announces the ***Winner***
